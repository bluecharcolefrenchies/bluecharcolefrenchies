About us

It all started with Stanley.

Our very first Frenchie’s affectionate, playful nature captured our hearts, and our breeding program grew from there. We named it after our boys, Charlie and Cole — hence the spelling.

As a boutique hobby breeder and proud CKC members in Aurora, Ontario, we raise every puppy in our home, surrounded by the sights and sounds of family life. We believe in quality over quantity — and when you bring home one of our pups, you become part of our extended family for life.

Guardian Home Program

Every one of our dogs deserves a family of their own.

Guardian homes are an important part of our program. As we continue to grow, they make sure each of our breeding dogs gets to live their best life: at home with a family who adores them, with a couch to call their own and plenty of one-on-one love.

In fact, most of our girls and boys currently live with guardian families, and we couldn’t be more grateful to the people who make that possible.

Curious about what a guardian home entails?

Every so often, a puppy stands out with exceptional qualities that we’d love to keep in our breeding program. Rather than keeping them with us, we offer these extraordinary pups to approved guardian homes at a reduced price, so they can grow up as a cherished family dog while staying part of our program.

If you live in or around the GTA and would like to learn more, we’d love to hear from you.
